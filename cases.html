INTERNAL PRODUCT / COMMERCE

OMIX STORE

Problem space: A marketplace has to coordinate buyers, sellers, listings, orders, payments, delivery and support without losing the financial history of a transaction.

Engineering focus: Seller onboarding, catalogue and order lifecycles, payment initiation and callback handling, ledger records, affiliate attribution, and operational reconciliation.

Key design question: How should each participant’s balance and order state change when payment providers, sellers and delivery workflows operate asynchronously?

INTERNAL PRODUCT / BUSINESS OPERATIONS

AIDE

Problem space: Small organizations often manage records and routine operations through disconnected paperwork, spreadsheets and messaging threads.

Engineering focus: Structured records, role-aware workflows, local data capture, synchronization behavior and clear operational summaries.

Key design question: Which tasks can be completed reliably on a device during intermittent connectivity, and how should conflicts be resolved when it reconnects?

INTERNAL PRODUCT / AI INFRASTRUCTURE

BIFROST

Problem space: Applications and agents that use multiple AI providers can accumulate separate credentials, request formats, routing rules and failure paths.

Engineering focus: Provider adapters, model aliases, routing policy, request validation, timeout handling, compatible fallbacks and usage visibility.

Key design question: How can an application change model providers without spreading provider-specific behavior across every feature?

Explore Bifrost →Model routing guide →
INTERNAL PRODUCT / EDUCATION

DECIMAL

Problem space: School operations connect learners, staff, classes, timetables, assessments, administration and reporting. Separate tools can create duplicated or inconsistent records.

Engineering focus: Shared academic records, permissions, timetable constraints, administrative workflows and reporting boundaries.

Key design question: How should a change to a learner, class or timetable propagate across dependent workflows without creating contradictory records?
