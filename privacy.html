PRIVACY POLICY

This policy explains how OMIX Digital Solutions handles information when you visit omixsystems.store or the OMIX Journal at blog.omixsystems.store, contact us, or use features on those public websites.

1. Who operates these websites
OMIX Digital Solutions (“OMIX”, “we”, “us”) operates omixsystems.store and blog.omixsystems.store from Kenya. For privacy questions, contact omixsystems@gmail.com.
2. Information you provide
Depending on the feature you use, you may provide your name, email address, telephone number, organization, project requirements, messages, feedback or other information you choose to submit. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details or sensitive personal information through general contact forms.
3. Technical information
When a website is requested, our hosting, security and delivery providers may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the service. This can include IP address, browser and device information, requested URL, referring page, timestamps and diagnostic logs. We use this information for delivery, security, troubleshooting and reliability.
4. Cookies and local storage
The websites may use essential browser storage to remember interface preferences, such as the selected colour theme, and to support features you request. Your browser provides controls to inspect, restrict or clear stored data. If optional analytics or other non-essential technologies are introduced, this policy and any required consent controls will be updated before they are used.
5. Advertising and third-party vendors
If advertising is enabled on the OMIX Journal, Google and other advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure and personalize advertisements, subject to their own policies and your applicable settings. Google’s information about how it uses data from partner sites is available at Google’s partner-sites policy. You can review Google ad personalization settings at Google Ads Settings. We will not represent advertising as active unless it has actually been enabled.
6. Why information is used
We use submitted information to answer enquiries, prepare requested proposals, provide support, operate website features, protect the websites, diagnose faults and improve our services. We do not use general contact-form submissions to send unrelated marketing unless you have asked to receive it or another lawful basis applies.
7. Service providers and sharing
We may share limited information with hosting, email, form-processing, security, analytics or advertising providers where they are needed to operate a feature or service. Those providers process information under their own terms and applicable arrangements. We do not sell personal information.
8. Retention and security
We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to maintain business records, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.
9. Your choices and requests
You may ask us to access, correct or delete personal information you have provided, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements. You can also clear browser storage, manage cookies through your browser and use the advertising controls linked above. Contact us at omixsystems@gmail.com to make a request.
10. Children and other OMIX products
These public websites are not designed to collect personal information from children. Separate OMIX applications and third-party services may have their own privacy notices and terms; review the notice presented by the relevant product before submitting information there.
11. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy when website functionality, providers or legal requirements change. The date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.

This page describes the intended practices for the public websites. Before enabling advertising, analytics or new form providers, OMIX must verify that the deployed configuration matches these disclosures and update them where necessary.
