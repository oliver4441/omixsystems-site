SaaS Platform Development in Kenya

A practical overview of SaaS platform architecture, multi-tenancy, authentication, billing, deployment and operations from OMIX Digital Solutions.

SaaS platforms require a different architecture from a simple marketing website. The application normally needs user accounts, authorization, tenant or organization boundaries, persistent data, billing, notifications, monitoring and a repeatable deployment process.

OMIX Digital Solutions builds SaaS and platform software around those operational requirements. The implementation can include multi-tenant application architecture, authentication and roles, subscription or usage billing, APIs, dashboards and cloud deployment.

Core SaaS concerns

  • Tenant isolation and authorization
  • Authentication and account lifecycle
  • Application and database architecture
  • Billing and subscription workflows
  • Backups, monitoring and deployment
  • Performance and maintainability

Kenyan payment and operating context

Some Kenyan SaaS products also need local payment integrations. M-PESA APIs can be connected to application workflows so that payment events can trigger account changes, receipts, service activation or internal reconciliation.

A SaaS product should be designed around the actual workflow rather than only its interface. OMIX therefore treats product requirements, architecture, deployment and ongoing operations as connected parts of the platform.
