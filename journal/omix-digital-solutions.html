OMIX Digital Solutions

An overview of OMIX Digital Solutions, a Kenya-based software company building SaaS platforms, business systems, APIs and digital infrastructure.

OMIX Digital Solutions is a software company based in Kenya. The company builds and operates software products, SaaS platforms, business systems, websites, APIs and digital infrastructure.

OMIX combines product development with client engineering. Its work spans commerce, education, business operations, analytics and AI infrastructure. The focus is on systems that can be deployed, operated and maintained after launch.

What OMIX builds

  • SaaS platforms and multi-tenant applications
  • Business management and workflow systems
  • Web applications and progressive web applications
  • APIs, payment integrations and webhooks
  • Dashboards and data systems
  • Cloud deployment and operational infrastructure

Built in Kenya

OMIX is based in Kericho, Kenya and delivers projects remotely. Its engineering work can support organizations that need software for local workflows, including M-PESA-based payments and offline-ready applications, as well as products intended for users beyond Kenya.
