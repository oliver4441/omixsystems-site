Business Systems & Digital Infrastructure in Kenya

How OMIX builds business systems, APIs, dashboards, offline-ready applications and cloud infrastructure for organizations in Kenya.

Business software becomes infrastructure when an organization depends on it for daily operations. Examples include school management, commerce, customer workflows, payments, reporting, internal administration and data processing.

OMIX Digital Solutions builds business systems around these operational requirements. Projects can combine web applications, APIs, databases, dashboards, offline-ready interfaces and cloud infrastructure.

Operational systems

  • Workflow and process management
  • Role-based administrative dashboards
  • Customer and organization records
  • Payment and integration workflows
  • Reporting and analytics
  • Offline-ready progressive web applications

Infrastructure as part of the product

Digital infrastructure includes the services required to keep an application available and maintainable: hosting, domains, deployment pipelines, databases, backups, monitoring and access controls.

For Kenyan organizations, infrastructure choices also need to account for connectivity, payment services, operating costs and the skills available to maintain the system. OMIX designs software with these practical constraints in mind.
